TASIN KABIR — PROFESSIONAL PORTFOLIO

This package contains the complete static website for Tasin Kabir, Digital Marketer and Founder of BoostX Marketing Agency.

IMPORTANT
- The Portfolio section presents the included portfolio documents as CLIENT WORK.
- The Case Studies section is based on those actual client-work deliverables (Bhumi’s Facebook marketing, Sportskeeda Football Instagram marketing, Facebook Ads, Texas restaurant lead generation, keyword research, financial-planning email marketing, and data-entry/research).
- Case studies explain the challenge, work completed, strategic reasoning and relevant KPIs without inventing unsupported client results.
- The documents open from the website using the included PDF files.
- The contact section includes email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp links with recognizable contact icons.
- Keep the folder structure unchanged when uploading to GitHub Pages.
